IMSC Summer Camp

International Maritime Sport Community (IMSC) is a volunteer-based organisation that promotes an active and healthy lifestyle among maritime professionals worldwide.

IMSC Summer Camp is a sports event for seafarers, people working in the maritime industry, and their families to spend time together in an active, healthy, and positive environment.

The Summer Camp logo combines symbols of summer, sport, and the sea to reflect the maritime context of the event.
